Watch a 40-minute lecture in 20 minutes. Press + to speed up any video and - to slow it down. No digging through menus, and it works on sites that have no speed control at all.

WHAT IT'S GOOD FOR
• Lectures and courses. Move through the easy parts at 2x, then drop back for the hard ones.
• Tutorials and talks. Skim ahead, slow down when it gets to the part you came for.
• Any site with a video. YouTube, course platforms, news sites, and players embedded in blogs.

HOW IT WORKS
• Each press of + or - changes the speed by 0.25x by default. You don't need to hold Shift.
• The speed shows on the video, up to 16x. Click it to jump back to 1x.
• The settings page keeps a running total of the time you've saved.
• Pick your own keys and step. Show the control on hover, keep it on screen, or hide it, in any of six spots.

GET STARTED
1. Reload any tab that already had a video open.
2. Press + while a video plays.
That's all the setup there is.

On a page with an embedded player, click the player once so it gets your key presses. The keys do nothing while you type in a search box or comment field.

FREE AND PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no analytics, no server. Video Speed Tool collects nothing about you or the pages you visit. Your settings sync between your own browsers through your browser's sync, and the time-saved total stays on this device. The code is open source under the MIT license. Privacy policy: https://github.com/gsajith/youtube-speed-tool/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

WHY IT ASKS FOR ACCESS TO ALL WEBSITES
Videos can be on any site, so the extension runs on every page. On each one it finds the videos, changes their speed when you press a speed key, and draws the speed control over them. It checks each key press only to see if it's one of your two speed keys. It doesn't read page text, forms or your history.

GOOD TO KNOW
Audio may cut out above 4x. Browsers do that, not this extension.
