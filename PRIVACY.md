# Privacy policy for Video Speed Tool

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Video Speed Tool collects no personal data. It has no analytics, no account and no server. It doesn't sell or share anything with anyone.

## What it stores

- **Your settings.** Your two shortcut keys, the speed step, and how and where the on-video control appears. The browser saves these with its sync storage. If you're signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser copies them to your other devices through your Google or Mozilla account. The extension never sees that account or anything in it.
- **Time saved.** One number: the total seconds you've saved by watching faster. It stays on this device and is never synced.

## What it reads on web pages

The extension runs on every website, because videos can appear on any site. On each page it does only this:

- It finds video elements and changes their playback speed when you press a speed key or click the control.
- It draws the speed control over the video.
- It checks each key press only to see if it's one of your two speed keys. It ignores and keeps no other keys.

It doesn't read page text, forms, cookies or browsing history. It sends nothing over the network.

## Deleting your data

Click "Reset to defaults" on the settings page to clear your settings. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on this device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The repository's commit history keeps every earlier version.
